THE MIDNIGHT QUILL - A Gloamwick Academy Mystery
=================================================

HOW TO PLAY
-----------
1. Unzip this folder anywhere.
2. Double-click index.html (any modern browser: Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari).
   No server, install, or internet connection is needed.
3. Click the title screen or press ENTER to begin. (Sound starts on that first click.)

CONTROLS
--------
WASD / Arrow keys .... move
E / Space / Enter .... talk, examine, advance dialogue
1, 2, 3 .............. cast spells once learned
   1 Lucerna (light, toggle on/off)
   2 Reseris (unlock doors and gates beside you)
   3 Levitas (lift heavy things near you)
Up/Down + E, or 1-4 .. pick dialogue choices
H .................... get a hint for your next step
M .................... mute / unmute music

THE STORY
---------
It is past curfew at Gloamwick Academy. Professor Pimm's Whisperwrit Quill, which
writes down every honest word spoken near it, has been stolen, and a trail of
glowing footprints leads off into the dark. Sneak past Mr. Gristle, question your
classmates, cast spells, gather clues, and unmask the culprit.

TIPS
----
- Mr. Gristle patrols the corridor and the library. A lit wand can be seen from far
  away, so switch Lucerna off and hide in shadowy alcoves (darker floor patches)
  when he walks past. If he catches you, you are sent back to the Common Room.
- How you treat people matters. Bram and Nib are friendlier if you are kind.
- You need at least four clues that point to the culprit before you can accuse.
- Two endings are available, depending on how you handle the confession.

Play time: roughly 15-30 minutes. Everything (art, music, sound) is generated in code.
